✦ Why We Exist

Building Software Real Estate Operators Actually Love

For decades, property management platforms were clunky, rigid, and slow. Teams were forced to juggle disjointed point solutions for rent collection, work orders, background checks, and bookkeeping.

PrimeStay was born out of a simple conviction: property managers, landlords, and residents deserve an elegant, AI-augmented CRM that works seamlessly out of the box — liberating operators from administrative chaos so they can focus on high-yield portfolio expansion.

✦ Our Journey

From Concept to Global Standard

How PrimeStay expanded from a local California pilot to powering real estate operators across 48 nations.

2022

The Inception & First 5,000 Units

Elena & Marcus founded PrimeStay after experiencing catastrophic ledger sync bugs in legacy tools. Built the core automated ACH engine and launched beta in San Francisco.

2023

Series A & AI Maintenance Launch

Raised $18M Series A led by Horizon PropTech Ventures. Released our proprietary Computer Vision maintenance diagnostic engine, slashing dispatch times by 54%.

2024

Global Expansion & Enterprise IoT

Opened our London office and expanded throughout the UK and EU. Integrated with smart lock ecosystems and major accounting suites (QuickBooks, Xero, MRI).

2026

400,000 Units & Next-Gen Real Estate OS

Surpassed $4.2B in annual rent volume processed with 99.98% uptime. PrimeStay is recognized as the leading autonomous CRM for modern property professionals.
