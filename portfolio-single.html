1. Client Overview & Regional Footprint

Apex Urban Living is a high-growth multifamily investment and asset management operator headquartered in Austin, Texas. Over a 36-month period, Apex expanded its portfolio from 850 units to over 3,400 Class-A and Class-B residential units spanning Texas, Colorado, Arizona, and Georgia.

2. The Challenge: Fragmented Legacy Drag

As the portfolio tripled in size, Apex's operational infrastructure broke under the strain of four disconnected legacy software tools:

72-Hour Repair Latency

Maintenance tickets sat in email queues, requiring manual property manager phone tag with local plumbers and electricians.

Manual Rent Reconciliations

Accounting spent 11 business days each month reconciling disparate check payments, ACH reversals, and paper lockboxes.

Tenant Churn at 38%

Frustrated residents left negative reviews online due to slow communication, causing vacancy marketing costs to spike.

3. The PrimeStay Solution

In Q2, Apex deployed PrimeStay Enterprise across all 3,400 units, replacing four disparate systems with one autonomous platform:

  • Autonomous Rent Rails: 100% of residents onboarded to next-day digital ACH and mobile pay, with automated late-rule enforcement and real-time ledger balance updates.
  • Computer Vision Maintenance Triage: Tenants upload 5-second video clips of plumbing/electrical faults. PrimeStay automatically identifies required replacement parts and dispatches preferred contractors within 4 minutes.
  • Multi-Entity Investor Reporting: 28 distinct ownership LLCs receive automated monthly owner distributions, 1099 exports, and unit-level P&L dashboards.
